## How to accept

Comment on any Luxir pull request or issue with exactly:

    I have read the Luxir CLA and I hereby sign it.

Sign from the GitHub account your commits are attributed to (the commit
author email must be linked to that account). Your GitHub login, account id,
the comment link, and the time are recorded publicly on the `cla-signatures`
branch of the repository.

If you contribute on behalf of an employer that holds rights in your work
(section 4), contact the Steward before your pull request can be merged.
